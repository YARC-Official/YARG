At 44.1 kHz with a 2048-point FFT, bin spacing is 21.53 Hz (44100 / 2048). A musical note like Concert A (440.0 Hz) lands between Bin 20 (430.66 Hz) and Bin 21 (452.19 Hz). 📐 Magnitude, Phase, and the STFT Vector Sum:
• Magnitude (Vector Length): Over the 60 ms window, the FFT integrates the signal against reference cosine and sine basis waves. In-phase energy adds constructively into a 2D complex vector (Real, Imaginary). Magnitude = √(R² + I²).
• Phase (Vector Angle): The angle θ = atan2(Imag, Real) specifies the wave's peak position relative to the center of the 60 ms window.
• Sub-Bin Tracking: A single 60 ms window cannot separate 440 Hz from 443 Hz with precision. Comparing phase between Frame 1 and Frame 2 across the 15 ms hop interval resolves exact frequency continuously.
